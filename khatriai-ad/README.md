# KhatriAI sparrow film

A 26-second vertical (9:16) launch ad for **ManitPF KhatriAI**, modelled on the sparrow
film for the Claude Fable 5.1 launch. Every frame and note is generated by code. The
bird, the plant, the archival plates, the dithered rings and the music are all drawn or
synthesized on the page; there are no image, video or audio assets.

- `index.html` plays the film in a browser, with sound, scrubbing and restart.
- `render.cjs` exports it to `khatriai-sparrow-film.mp4` (1080×1920, 30 fps, AAC audio).
- `khatriai-sparrow-film.mp4` is the rendered ad.

## How it works

Every shot is a pure function of time `t`. `render(t)` poses the SVG sparrow and lily,
selects a montage plate, and redraws the dithered aperture. The aperture is drawn at
quarter resolution with a 4×4 Bayer matrix and scaled up with nearest-neighbour
filtering. Live playback and frame-exact export share this one code path.

The five archival plates are generated once at load:

- currant sprigs trained along the mark;
- a cyanotype blueprint with dimensions;
- an island chart built from a noisy distance field around the mark;
- a star atlas;
- a shaded scanning-electron-micrograph pass.

The score is in A major at 100 BPM, built from pads, a music-box arpeggio, a kick under
the montage, shutter clicks on each cut, and a wing flutter synced to every downstroke.
It is synthesized with Web Audio and rendered through an `OfflineAudioContext` for export.

## Re-rendering

```sh
npm i playwright
FFMPEG=/path/to/ffmpeg node render.cjs khatriai-sparrow-film.mp4
```

`PAGES` sets how many browser pages capture in parallel (default 4). `FPS=60` doubles
the frame rate.
